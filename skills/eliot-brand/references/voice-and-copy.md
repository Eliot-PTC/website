# Eliot voice and copy

## Who we're talking to

Eliot families, teachers and staff, grandparents, neighbors and local businesses. Most read on a phone, between other things. Many are busy; some are new to the school or to English. Write so a quick glance gets the point.

## Personality

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Warm and grateful | Gushing or sugary |
| Upbeat and energetic | Frantic or pushy |
| Community-minded ("we", "together") | Institutional ("the organization") |
| Clear and concrete | Vague or jargony |
| Inclusive | Assuming everyone can give money |

## Patterns that work

**Big short headline, then the details.**
> COFFEE DAY! / TODAY! 7:30–9:30 AM AT ELIOT

**Gratitude first.**
> Thank you, Eliot supporters! Every gift makes a difference.

**Progress, framed as a shared goal.**
> So close! Let's reach our $15,000 goal! Every gift brings us closer.

**Triplets for closing lines.**
> A little coffee. A lot of appreciation. A stronger Eliot.

**Action-label cards:** a short handwritten-style heading plus one plain sentence.
> **Buy yourself a cup!** Stop by the coffee truck and enjoy a coffee or specialty drink.

**Impact through real stories.** Use a real teacher quote only with their permission, with their name and role, e.g. "2nd Grade Teacher, Eliot School".

## Recurring lines (on-brand, reusable)

- Together, we make Eliot stronger.
- Every gift makes a difference.
- The more we raise, the more we can do.
- Give this weekend. Make more possible.
- Let's do more for our students and classrooms.

## Why we fundraise (approved themes)

- More resources so students can see themselves in the classroom.
- More support for different learning needs.
- More opportunities to create welcoming, engaging classrooms for every student.
- Teacher grants that fund books, flexible seating and classroom projects.

## Practical rules

- Dates: "Sunday, October 25th" in headlines is fine; in body text use "Sunday, Oct. 25". Always double-check the day matches the date.
- Times: "4:00–5:30 PM" with an en dash, PM in caps.
- Money: "$15,000", "$2.6K" only in big stat callouts.
- Always say how to act: the Givebutter link or QR code, a sign-up link, or a contact.
- Include a no-cost way to help where it fits (volunteer, attend, share the post), so every family can take part.
- Don't invent numbers, quotes, names, prizes or deadlines. Leave a clear placeholder like [DATE] and ask.
- Don't name or single out individual donors or children without permission. No photos of identifiable students unless the PTC confirms consent.
- Third-party logos (sponsors, Amazon, Givebutter) only when the partnership is real and approved.
