PTC Events Through the Year

These are the community events Eliot families look forward to every year. Exact dates for 2026–27 will be posted here and in the Facebook group.

September

Welcome Back Night

Kick off the year together; last year was an outdoor movie night.

October

Curriculum Night

Meet the teachers and hear from the principal and specialists.

October

Trunk or Treat

Costumes, treats, and decorated cars. Families can host a trunk.

January

STEM Festival

Hands-on science and engineering for every grade.

Winter

Winter Dance Party

A family dance night to beat the January blues.

March

Multicultural Fair

Food, music, and traditions from Eliot families around the world.

Spring

Parents Night Out

An adults-only evening that raises money for the school.

May

Staff Kickball Game

Eliot takes on Sunita Williams in a friendly rivalry game.

June

End of Year Picnic

A relaxed send-off to summer for the whole community.
