Ways to volunteer

Have one hour or ten? There's a job that fits. Email us and we'll match you up.

About 2 hrs / month

Room parent

Be the link between your child's teacher and class families. Organize class parties and share reminders.

One 2-hr shift

Book fair helper

Help kids find books, run the register, and restock shelves during the November fair.

Event day

Festival crew

Set up tables, staff an activity booth, or help clean up after the Harvest Festival.

Flexible

Hospitality committee

Plan staff lunches and Teacher Appreciation Week so our teachers know they're valued.
